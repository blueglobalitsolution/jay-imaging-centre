MRI Procedure Guidelines

When is it conducted?

To identify micro-structural configurations, joint cartilage problems, complex brain imaging profiles, vascular structures, and soft tissue configurations.

Patient Guidelines & Preparation:

  1. Remove all metallic items (jewelry, eyeglasses, keys, watch, dentures) prior to tracking fields.
  2. Inform the technician immediately if you have standard pacemakers, orthopedic plates, or metallic implants.
  3. The process is radiation-free and completely painless. Standard procedures take between 15 to 45 minutes.

CT Scan Procedure Guidelines

When is it conducted?

To evaluate complex bone fractures, internal abdominal pathologies, organ infections, sudden internal bleeding, or tracking oncology developments.

Patient Guidelines & Preparation:

  1. You will change into a standardized medical gown and remove any interfering external metal items.
  2. For contrast-enhanced scans (IV or Oral PO contrast), fasting for 4 to 6 hours before arrival may be mandatory.
  3. An intercom system enables safe, continuous two-way communication with your technician throughout the fast imaging cross-section scan.
×

Sonography Procedure Guidelines

When is it conducted?

To evaluate kidney/gallbladder stone anomalies, track obstetric fetus progression parameters, monitor visceral soft-tissue masses, and view color Doppler blood directional vectors.

Patient Guidelines & Preparation:

  1. Abdominal evaluation: Fasting for at least 6 hours is highly recommended for proper visualization.
  2. Pelvic/Obstetric tracking: Drink abundant water 1 hour prior to your scan to keep the urinary bladder full.
  3. A water-soluble gel will be applied over the target area to smoothly slide the wave transducer without discomfort.
×

Digital X-Ray Guidelines

When is it conducted?

Primarily utilized to screen skeletal configurations for injury, observe respiratory fields, track joint degeneration conditions, or perform structural dynamic assessments (IVP/HSG).

Patient Guidelines & Preparation:

  1. Wear comfortable loose clothing. You may be requested to shift to a clean patient gown to safeguard structural detail clear printing.
  2. Ensure all localized metallic objects are isolated to prevent dynamic visual shadows.
  3. Inform our clinical support professionals explicitly if there is a possibility of active pregnancy.
